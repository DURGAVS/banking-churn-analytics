### Planted data quality issues

You are meant to find and fix these:

1. **649 duplicate rows**
2. **Inconsistent gender labels** — 8 different spellings: `Male`, `M`, `male`, `m`, `Female`, `F`, `female`, `f`
3. **Missing values** — credit_score (2,110), estimated_salary (1,710), monthly_logins (1,312), age (900)
4. **Whitespace and case noise** in `geography` — some values are `" France "` or `"GERMANY"`
5. **`balance` stored as text** — about 6% of rows have a `€` prefix, so the column reads as string
6. **90 impossible ages** — values of 0, 1, 3, 199, 250

`customers_clean_ANSWER_KEY.csv` is the correct version. **Do your cleaning first, then compare.** Don't peek early.

## 6. `monthly_activity.csv.gz` — 1,200,000 rows

customer_id, activity_month, num_transactions, total_debit, total_credit, avg_balance

12 months (Sep 2025 – Aug 2026) per customer. Gzipped — pandas reads it directly with `pd.read_csv(path)`.

**This is your window-function table.** LAG, running totals, moving averages, rank within branch.
Note: customers who churned show declining activity over time. That pattern is real and findable.
